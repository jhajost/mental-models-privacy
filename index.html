Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 March 2026

Mental Models Trainer ("the app") is a free, offline Android application. This policy describes what data the app handles.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no network requests.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your study progress (spaced-repetition card scores) and investing checklist sessions in your device's local storage (AsyncStorage / SQLite). This data never leaves your device.

Third-party services

The Library screen contains Amazon Associates affiliate links. Tapping a link opens your device's browser; Amazon's own privacy policy then applies. The app itself sends no data to Amazon or any other third party.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue or contact us via the app's store listing.
